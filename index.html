Inspired by the Sea

Every hat carries a little piece of the coast.

From early-morning waves to warm sunsets and salty summer nights.

We started on a stretch of the Corniche where the sea wall is still warm at midnight and the fishing boats go out before anyone else is awake. The first hat was cut to keep the sun off one person’s face on that wall. The second was for a friend who asked where the first one came from.

Everything is drawn here, dyed here and stitched here. The lighthouse on our patch has not stood for six hundred years, and we put it on anyway — because this city is mostly a story about what the water gives back.
